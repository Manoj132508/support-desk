# Delivery and tracking

Delivery options, timings, and what to do when a parcel does not arrive.

## Delivery options

Standard delivery arrives in 3 to 5 working days and is free on orders over
£40. Express delivery arrives the next working day if ordered before 4pm and
costs £5.95. We do not deliver on Sundays or bank holidays.

## Tracking your order

A tracking number is emailed when the order is dispatched and also appears on
the order page. Tracking can take up to 12 hours to show its first scan, which
is normal and does not mean the parcel is lost.

## If your parcel has not arrived

If a parcel is more than 3 working days past its estimated delivery date,
report it and we will open an investigation with the carrier. Investigations
take up to 5 working days. If the parcel is not found we will replace or refund
it.

## Delivery address changes

A delivery address can be changed while an order is still being prepared. Once
the order is dispatched the address is fixed with the carrier and we cannot
change it.

## Missed deliveries

The carrier will attempt delivery twice. After a second failed attempt the
parcel is held at a local depot for 7 days, then returned to us and refunded
automatically, minus the original delivery charge.
