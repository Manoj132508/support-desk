# Returns and refunds

How to return an item and when to expect your money back.

## Return window

You have 30 days from the delivery date to start a return. The item must be
unused and in its original packaging. Personalised items cannot be returned
unless they arrived faulty.

## Starting a return

Open the order in your account and choose "Return items". You will be given a
prepaid label to print. Parcels must be dropped off within 14 days of the label
being issued, after which it expires and you will need a new one.

## When a refund is issued

Refunds are issued once the returned item reaches our warehouse and passes
inspection, which usually takes 2 working days from delivery back to us. The
refund is made to the original payment method and takes a further 3 to 5
working days to appear.

## Faulty items

If an item arrives faulty or damaged, the 30-day window does not apply and
return postage is always free. Report it within 6 months of delivery. We will
either replace the item or refund it in full, including the original delivery
charge.

## What is not refunded

Original delivery charges are not refunded when you return an item because you
changed your mind. They are refunded in full when the item was faulty, wrongly
described, or not what you ordered.
