# Cancelling an order

This article explains when an order can be cancelled and what happens
afterwards. It is the article the assistant cites most often, because
cancellation is the one action it can propose.

## Before an order is dispatched

An order can be cancelled at any point before it is dispatched. Cancellation is
immediate and no charge is taken. If payment has already been authorised, the
authorisation is released within 3 to 5 working days, depending on the bank.

You can cancel from the order page, or by asking our support assistant.

## After an order is dispatched

Once an order has been dispatched it can no longer be cancelled, because the
parcel has already left the warehouse and is with the carrier. This applies
even if the tracking information has not updated yet.

If you no longer want a dispatched order, refuse delivery or return it once it
arrives. See "Returns and refunds" for how to start a return.

## After an order is delivered

A delivered order cannot be cancelled. Delivery completes the order, so what
you are asking for at that point is a return, not a cancellation.

## Orders containing personalised items

Personalised and made-to-order items enter production as soon as the order is
placed. They cannot be cancelled after the first hour, even if the rest of the
order has not been dispatched.

## How long a cancellation takes to show

A cancellation is applied to the order immediately. Your bank may take a
further 3 to 5 working days to show the released authorisation on your
statement. The order status in your account is the accurate record.
